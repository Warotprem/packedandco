Questions people ask

Why buy here and not a cheaper kit on Amazon?

Fair question: some kits are cheaper and arrive faster. Ours comes with a cordless pump that switches itself off, bags sized for a carry-on, and a real person who answers your email within a business day. If you're flying in the next fortnight, a local store will be quicker.

Will this let me pack more weight?

No. It saves space, not weight. Your clothes weigh the same once they're compressed, and many airlines weigh carry-on (on Jetstar, for example, it's usually 7 kg), so check the allowance for your fare. Use the extra room for bulky jumpers, or for the souvenirs on the way home.

Will my clothes come out wrinkled?

They can crease. Roll each item before it goes in the bag, then hang everything up when you arrive. Knits, jumpers and jackets come out best.

How do I repack for the trip home?

Pack the pump in your carry-on. At the hotel, refill the bags, zip them and hold the pump on the valve until it stops.

Can I take the pump on the plane?

It has a small built-in lithium battery. Devices like this generally go in carry-on, not checked bags, but rules vary, so check with your airline.

Do the bags leak?

Zip each bag fully along its length before pumping. If a bag arrives faulty, email us and we'll replace it or refund you.
