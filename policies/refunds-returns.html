Refunds & Returns Policy

We want you to be happy with your Packed & Co. order. This page explains what we do and don't offer, and what your rights are under Australian law.

Our guarantee under Australian law

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

Nothing in this policy limits those rights.

Change-of-mind returns

We're sorry, but we don't offer refunds or returns for change of mind. That includes ordering the wrong size or colour, or simply deciding you no longer want an item. Because our products ship from overseas, returning them for change of mind isn't practical.

Personalised (custom name) items are made just for you, so they can't be returned for change of mind either.

Please read the product description, and check any personalised name carefully, before you place your order.

When something is wrong

If your item is faulty, damaged, not as described, or we sent the wrong item or the wrong name, we'll put it right. This applies to personalised items too.

  • Major failure: you choose either a refund or a replacement.
  • Minor failure: we'll repair, replace or refund the item.

If we printed a name differently from how you typed it at checkout, that's our mistake and we'll fix it.

How to make a claim

Please email hello@packedandco.com within a reasonable time of noticing the problem and include:

  1. your order number
  2. a short description of the problem
  3. photos showing the problem if you can (and the name you ordered, for personalised items). If photos aren't possible, tell us what happened and we'll work it out with you

We'll reply, let you know what happens next, and work out the right remedy with you.

Do I need to send the item back?

For low-value faulty items, we usually won't ask you to send the item back.

If we do need the item back, we'll tell you where to send it and we'll pay the return postage. Please don't post anything back before we've been in touch, so that it isn't lost.

Refunds

Once a refund is approved, we'll send it to your original payment method within 5 business days. Your bank or card provider may take a few extra days to show it in your account.

Late or lost parcels

If your tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, or your order hasn't arrived within 25 business days of shipping, contact us and we'll resend your order or give you a refund. See our Shipping Policy for details.

Contact

Email hello@packedandco.com with your order number and we'll help you as quickly as we can.
