Frequently Asked Questions

Shipping and orders

How long will my order take to arrive? We process orders in 1–3 business days. Delivery then usually takes 7–14 business days to metro Australia, or up to 20 business days to regional areas. Personalised (custom name) items need an extra 2–4 business days to make. Our products ship from our supplier's warehouse overseas, which is why delivery takes a bit longer than a local parcel. Shipping is free on all orders, and we currently deliver to Australian addresses only. See our Shipping Policy for details.

How do I track my order? When your order ships, we'll email you a tracking number. Tracking can take a few days to show movement at first, which is normal. If your tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, or your order hasn't arrived within 25 business days of shipping, email us and we'll resend it or give you a refund.

Vacuum compression bags

Will vacuum bags let me pack more weight? No. Vacuum bags save space, not weight. Your clothes weigh the same once they're compressed, and it's easy to fit more into your bag than your airline allows. Please check your airline's weight limits before you fly. Many airlines weigh carry-on (on Jetstar, for example, it's usually 7 kg), so check the allowance for your fare and route.

Will my clothes get wrinkled? They may crease a little. To keep creases down, roll your clothes before compressing them. When you arrive, unpack them and hang them up.

Does the pump fit in my luggage? Yes. The rechargeable pump is small enough to pack with your bags. We recommend keeping it in your carry-on so you can reseal your bags on the way home.

Can I take the pump on a plane? The pump has a small built-in lithium battery. Devices like this are generally allowed in carry-on under airline rules, but rules vary, so please check with your airline before you fly. Keep the pump in your carry-on rather than your checked bag.

How do I charge the pump? The pump charges by USB-C. A charging cable is included, but a wall charger isn't, so use a USB charger you already have.

What if airport security opens my bags? That's fine. Security staff may open compressed bags to check them. You can reseal them by hand, either with the pump or by rolling the bag to push the air out.

My pump isn't working. What should I do? Sorry about that! Email hello@packedandco.com with your order number and a short description or video of the problem. If it's faulty, we'll sort out a replacement, repair or refund in line with the Australian Consumer Law. For low-value faulty items, we usually won't ask you to send the item back.

Personalised name bags

What can I have printed on a name bag? We print the name exactly as you type it, so please double-check the spelling before you order. You can use letters (A–Z, a–z), numbers and spaces, up to 12 characters. The case is made from PU (not leather) and measures 21 × 14.5 × 16.5 cm.

My name bag has the wrong name on it. What now? If we printed something different from what you typed at checkout, that's our mistake and we'll fix it. Email hello@packedandco.com with your order number and a photo of the bag. Because we print names exactly as entered, we can't replace a bag for a spelling mistake made at checkout, so please check carefully before you order.

Returns and contact

Can I return something if I change my mind? We're sorry, but we don't offer change-of-mind returns, and personalised items can't be returned for change of mind. If your item is faulty, damaged, not as described, or we sent the wrong item or name, we'll make it right. Your Australian Consumer Law rights always apply. See our Refunds & Returns Policy.

How do I contact you? Email us at hello@packedandco.com and include your order number if you have one. We handle all customer support by email.
