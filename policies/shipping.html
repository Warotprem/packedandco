Shipping Policy

Thanks for shopping with Packed & Co. Here's exactly how and when your order gets to you.

Where we ship

We currently ship to Australian addresses only. All prices are in AUD.

Shipping cost

Shipping is free on every order. There's no minimum spend and no surprise fees at checkout. The cost of delivery is built into our prices.

Where your order ships from

Our products are sent from our supplier's warehouse overseas, not from a shop in Australia. That's why delivery takes longer than a local parcel, and we'd rather tell you that up front.

How long it takes

StepUsual timeframe
Order processing1–3 business days
Personalised (custom name) items: extra production timeadd 2–4 business days
Delivery to metro Australiausually 7–14 business days
Delivery to regional areasup to 20 business days

Business days are Monday to Friday, not counting public holidays. Timeframes start after processing (and production, for personalised items) is finished.

Example: a standard order to a capital city usually arrives about 8–17 business days after you order (1–3 days to dispatch, then 7–14 days in transit). A personalised order adds 2–4 business days, and regional areas can take up to 20 business days in transit.

These are our usual timeframes, not guarantees. Things outside our control, such as customs checks, courier delays, severe weather or busy holiday periods, can sometimes slow a parcel down.

Tracking your order

When your order ships, we'll email you a tracking number. Tracking can take a few days to show movement while your parcel is handed between carriers. That's normal.

If you think your tracking email should have arrived by now, check your spam folder, then contact us at hello@packedandco.com.

Late or lost parcels

Please contact us at hello@packedandco.com with your order number if:

  • your tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, or
  • your order hasn't arrived within 25 business days of shipping.

We'll look into it, then resend your order or give you a refund.

Getting your address right

Please check your shipping address carefully at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us at hello@packedandco.com as soon as possible. We can usually fix the address if the order hasn't been processed yet. Once a parcel has shipped, we may not be able to change where it goes.

Damaged, faulty or wrong items

If something arrives damaged or faulty, or isn't what you ordered, please see our Refunds & Returns Policy. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law always apply.

Contact

Questions about shipping? Email hello@packedandco.com and include your order number if you have one.
