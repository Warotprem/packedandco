Privacy Policy

Packed & Co. ("we", "us", "our") is a small online store based in Melbourne, Australia. We respect your privacy and handle your personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you place an order or contact us, we collect:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your shipping address
  • your phone number (required, because our courier needs it to deliver)
  • details of your order, such as the items you bought and any personalised name you entered

We may also collect information about how you use our website, through cookies and similar tools (see below).

Payment details

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details are handled by Stripe and are never seen or stored by us.

Why we collect it

We use your personal information to:

  • process, make and deliver your order
  • send you order confirmations, tracking details and updates
  • respond to your questions, claims and requests
  • understand how our website is used, and improve it
  • send you marketing emails, if you've agreed to receive them

Who we share it with

We only share what's needed to get your order to you:

  • Our supplier: we share your name, phone number, shipping address and order details so they can prepare and send your order. Our supplier is located overseas, so this information is sent outside Australia.
  • Couriers: your shipping details go to the courier delivering your parcel.
  • Stripe: processes your payment and order details (name, email, shipping address) on our behalf.

We don't sell your personal information. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Cookies and pixels

Our website doesn't currently use advertising pixels. Our checkout is run by Stripe, which uses cookies to process payments securely and prevent fraud. If we add analytics or advertising tools (for example from Meta or TikTok) we'll update this policy first.

You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies may stop the checkout from working properly.

Marketing emails

We'll only send you marketing emails if you've agreed to receive them. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time. You'll still get emails about your orders (such as tracking updates) after you unsubscribe.

Keeping your information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. We keep it only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires.

Access, correction and deletion

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@packedandco.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time. We may need to confirm your identity first. In some cases we may need to keep certain records, such as order records for tax purposes. If so, we'll explain why.

Questions and complaints

If you have a question or concern about your privacy, please email hello@packedandco.com and we'll do our best to resolve it. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
